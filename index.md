# Privacy Policy for Planets

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026

**Developer:** 4isper

## 1. Data Collection and Use

Planets is an application for browsing NASA's public Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) archive. The app does not require an account and does not ask users to provide personal information.

To display APOD content, the app retrieves public metadata and images from NASA-operated servers over the internet. These requests may expose your IP address and standard technical request information to NASA, as is typical for internet communications. NASA may process or log this information in accordance with its applicable privacy notices.

Planets does not store IP addresses, use them for tracking, or transmit personal information to servers operated by the developer.

Downloaded APOD records and images are cached on your device to support offline access. The app does not upload this cached content to the developer's servers. Depending on your device, operating system, and backup settings, some app data may be included in system backups.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Planets does not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking tools.

The app retrieves public content from NASA's servers. You may also choose to open external links, such as article pages or source references included in explanations. These links open in your browser, and the destination websites may process information according to their own privacy policies.

For more information, see NASA's privacy policy:

https://www.nasa.gov/privacy/

## 3. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

APOD records and images are stored locally for caching and offline access. The app does not maintain a developer-operated server containing users' personal information.

Cached content remains on your device until it is cleared, replaced, or removed with the app's data. You can clear the app's storage through your device settings or uninstall the application. Data included in system backups may be subject to your device's backup and deletion settings.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Planets does not require registration or ask users, including children, to provide personal information. The app provides access to public astronomy content.

If you have questions or concerns about children's privacy, please contact us using the details below.
